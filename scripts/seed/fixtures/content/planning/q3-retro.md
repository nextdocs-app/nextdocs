# Q3 retro

Archived. Themes, not a list of complaints.

## What went well

- The incident postmortem process produced action items that shipped.
- Two new engineers were on the on-call shadow rotation within a month.
- The design critique became a weekly habit.

## What did not

- The anomaly alert beta shipped with an 18% false-positive rate.
- The pricing experiment never started because the reconciliation report was late.
- Support was consulted after launch, not before.

## Actions

- Add a false-positive review to the beta checklist.
- Make the reconciliation report a dependency in the launch checklist template.
- Invite support to the launch readiness review.

## Next quarter

The theme is fewer launches, finished properly.

> Support should be part of launch readiness before a change reaches customers.

| Theme               | Evidence                         | Follow-up                          |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Alert quality       | 18% false-positive rate          | Add a review before beta expansion |
| Launch coordination | Support joined after launch      | Invite support to readiness review |
| Delivery focus      | Several bets remained unfinished | Limit concurrent launches          |

## Carry into next quarter

1. Review the false-positive rate before widening the alert beta.
2. Confirm the reconciliation report is ready before pricing work starts.
3. Check support readiness in the launch review.
