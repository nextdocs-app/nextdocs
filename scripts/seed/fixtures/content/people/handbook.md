# Employee handbook

Acme Inc builds Harbor, an analytics and event ingestion platform. This handbook explains how we work; the policies it links to are the authoritative versions.

## How we work

- Default to written decisions. A decision that lives only in a meeting will be re-litigated.
- Ship in small changes. A large change is a large rollback.
- Ask for help early. Nobody is expected to debug alone for a day.

## Working hours

We are distributed across four timezones. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 UTC. Meetings outside those hours need an agenda and a recording.

## Communication

- Async first in the project channel.
- Direct messages are for coordination, not decisions.
- Incidents use the incident channel, even when the fix is obvious.

## Reviews

Every engineer reviews code, and every engineer's code is reviewed. A review is about the change, never the author.

## Travel

Customer visits and team weeks are approved by the people lead. Book travel through the company account, not personally.

## Questions

Ask People Ops in the #people channel. If the question is about policy, ask before acting.

## Expenses

| Expense               | Limit                 | Approval    |
| --------------------- | --------------------- | ----------- |
| Team lunch            | 35 USD per person     | Manager     |
| Customer dinner       | 90 USD per person     | People lead |
| Home office equipment | 750 USD per year      | Manager     |
| Conference travel     | Actuals within budget | People lead |

Submit receipts within 30 days. If you are unsure whether something is reimbursable, ask before you spend, not after.

## Equipment

Every new joiner receives a laptop, a monitor, and a headset. Choose the operating system you are most productive on. Equipment is replaced every three years, or sooner if it fails; do not wait for a refresh cycle to report a broken machine.

## Feedback and growth

- One-to-ones happen every two weeks. The report sets the agenda; the manager keeps the notes.
- Feedback is specific, timely, and about behavior. "You missed the deadline and did not flag it" is useful; "you are unreliable" is not.
- Promotion is based on demonstrated scope, not tenure. The expectations for each level are in the career ladder, and managers discuss them before a review cycle, not during it.

## Leaving

Tell your manager before you tell the wider team. Hand over open work in writing, return equipment, and complete the exit interview. Access is removed on the last day; anything you need for your own records should be exported before then.

## Related policies

- PTO and leave.
- Remote work.
- Security and access control.
- Incident response.

These policies are maintained by their owners. Where this handbook and a policy disagree, the policy wins.

> Ask People Ops before making a policy exception; written guidance protects the next person too.
