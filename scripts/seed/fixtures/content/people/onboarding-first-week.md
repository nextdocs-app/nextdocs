# Onboarding: first week

The goal of week one is a working laptop, a clear first task, and a person to ask when something is confusing.

## Before day one

- People Ops sends the welcome note, laptop, and account invitations.
- The manager picks a buddy and a first task that can ship in the first two weeks.
- The team adds the new joiner to the project channel and the relevant space.

## Day one

- [ ] Sign in, set up the password manager, and enable two-factor authentication.
- [ ] Read the employee handbook and the security policy.
- [ ] Meet the manager for a 30-minute welcome and the plan for the week.
- [ ] Join the team stand-up and say hello.

## Days two to five

| Day | Focus | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Local setup | The app runs locally and the test suite passes |
| 3 | Product tour | The joiner can explain the core workflow in their own words |
| 4 | First task | A small change is open for review |
| 5 | Feedback | The joiner and manager agree on the next two weeks |

## Questions to ask at the end of the week

- What was slower than expected?
- Which document was missing or wrong?
- Who else should the joiner meet?

Update the onboarding checklist when an answer reveals a gap. The next joiner should not hit the same wall.
